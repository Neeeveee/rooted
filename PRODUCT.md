# Product

Derived from the approved PRD.md and DESIGN.md; those files remain authoritative.

## Register
product

## Users
Renters and plant beginners exploring how a plant fits into their furnished bedroom.

## Product Purpose
A single-screen P0 plant impact preview: place one plant, compare illustrative current and mature footprints, and distinguish space conflicts, pet safety, personal taste, and unknowns.

## Brand Personality
Calm, editorial, grounded.

## Anti-references
Plant shopping pages, admin dashboards, decorative landing pages, cartoon games, fake environmental heat maps.

## Design Principles
The room is the primary interface. Geometry responds to real state. Taste never overrides safety. Unknown evidence remains unknown. Scope stays within PRD P0.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text and icons accompany color. Visible keyboard focus, keyboard movement and click-to-place complement dragging. Responsive layout and reduced-motion support.
